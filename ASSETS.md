# BARBARIAN ARENA — Assets

**Art direction:** Bronze & Blood Arena. A readable third-person action screenshot set in a circular dirt arena at dusk. The player is intentionally made from simple blocky stone-and-cloth primitives; enemies are more detailed human barbarians with weathered skin, leather, fur, cloth, bronze details, and distinct weapon silhouettes. The environment uses iron-black rock, dry ochre earth, dark crimson banners, and restrained amber firelight. Arena Bronze `#B56B2B` is the signature color.
